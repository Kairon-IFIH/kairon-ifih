# 23. What if AI disappears?

**Is KAIRON still valuable without LLMs?** — Yes, because the core product is risk intelligence, financial quantification and optimization.

**What is the core non-AI product?** — Asset discovery + risk engine + regulatory mapping + financial-risk quantification + optimization.

**Can the regulatory layer operate with deterministic rules?** — Yes, especially for known regulatory requirements and mappings.

**Can the optimizer operate independently?** — Yes, the optimization engine does not fundamentally depend on an LLM.

**Can the risk engine operate independently?** — Yes, risk calculations can be rule-based and quantitative.

**Is AI an interface or a moat?** — AI is an intelligence layer; our long-term moat is the risk/financial/optimization data and decision infrastructure.

**What is the actual company beneath the AI hype?** — A financial risk decision and optimization platform for regulated institutions.
