# 27. Scalability

**Why can this become a billion-dollar company?** — Because the same risk-assurance platform can serve thousands of financial institutions across multiple countries and regulations.

**Can this scale beyond GIFT?** — Yes, GIFT is our beachhead, not our final market.

**Can this scale beyond India?** — Yes, the core platform stays the same while the regulatory layer changes by jurisdiction.

**Can the regulatory engine support new jurisdictions?** — Yes, we can add verified regulatory frameworks for each new market.

**Can the risk engine support multiple financial products?** — Yes, it can expand from banking into fintech, insurance, funds, brokers and other financial institutions.

**Can the quantum optimizer support larger problems?** — That is the long-term goal; we benchmark it against classical methods as problem size grows.

**Can we sell to 10 institutions?** — Yes, through high-touch enterprise pilots and direct sales.

**Can we sell to 100?** — Yes, once onboarding and integrations become standardized.

**Can we sell to 1,000?** — Potentially, through a scalable SaaS model, standardized integrations and channel partnerships.

**What becomes the global product?** — A global financial risk-assurance and capital-allocation platform.

**What is our TAM?** — The broader market is the global financial-institution cybersecurity, GRC, risk and compliance software market.

**What is the realistic serviceable market?** — Initially GIFT IFSC → India → major international financial centres.

**What percentage can we realistically capture?** — We should not claim a percentage yet; we need customer validation before making that forecast.

**Most important: "What does KAIRON become if it succeeds?"** — KAIRON becomes the financial industry's decision layer for understanding risk and deciding where capital should be deployed to reduce it.
