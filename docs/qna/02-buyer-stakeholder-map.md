# 2. Buyer & Stakeholder Map

**First customer:** A regulated financial institution in GIFT IFSC, starting with a bank or large fintech.

**Buyer:** The CISO or CRO who owns cyber and enterprise risk.

**Daily user:** Risk, cybersecurity and compliance teams.

**Technical approver:** CIO/CTO or CISO.

**Regulatory approver:** Compliance/Chief Risk Officer.

**Purchase blocker:** Usually the CIO, CISO, procurement or compliance team.

**Contract signer:** The institution's authorized procurement/business head.

**Financial beneficiary:** The institution, by reducing potential losses and using its risk budget better.

**Executive user:** CRO/CISO and senior management.

**Alerts go to:** Risk, security and compliance teams.

**Final approval:** The institution's authorized risk/compliance officer approves high-impact remediation actions.
