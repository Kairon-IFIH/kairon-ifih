# 4. Solution — What exactly is KAIRON?

**What does KAIRON do?** — KAIRON discovers cyber assets, identifies their regulatory and security risks, converts them into financial risk, and optimizes what to fix first.

**What happens when a customer logs in?** — They see their assets, risk exposure, regulatory gaps and the actions they should prioritize.

**What data enters the system?** — Asset data, security posture, regulatory requirements, business criticality, costs and risk information.

**What does KAIRON discover?** — It builds an inventory of the institution's important digital and cyber assets.

**What does KAIRON scan?** — It can assess endpoints, cloud assets, APIs, databases, certificates, critical systems and other connected assets.

**What is asset discovery?** — Simply, it means finding and understanding what digital assets the institution actually has.

**Does it discover endpoints?** — Yes, endpoints can be part of the asset inventory.

**Cloud assets?** — Yes, cloud infrastructure and workloads can be included.

**APIs?** — Yes, APIs can be mapped as part of the digital-asset inventory.

**Databases?** — Yes, especially databases holding critical or regulated information.

**Certificates?** — Yes, certificates can be tracked, particularly for security and cryptographic exposure.

**Cryptographic assets?** — Yes, we want to identify cryptographic dependencies to assess quantum-readiness.

**Third-party dependencies?** — Yes, important third-party systems and dependencies can be mapped.

**Critical financial infrastructure?** — Yes, we prioritize systems based on their importance to financial operations.

**How do we classify an asset?** — By factors like type, data sensitivity, business function, criticality and security exposure.

**How do we determine business criticality?** — We assess how severely the institution would be affected if that asset failed or was compromised.

**How do we map assets to regulations?** — KAIRON maps the asset's jurisdiction, function and data to the relevant regulatory requirements.

**How do we identify control gaps?** — We compare the required controls against the institution's current security and compliance posture.

**How do we calculate risk?** — We combine likelihood, impact, asset criticality, regulatory exposure and control gaps.

**How do we calculate financial exposure?** — We estimate the potential monetary impact of the identified risks using defined assumptions and evidence.

**How do we recommend remediation?** — KAIRON ranks possible actions by their cost, risk reduction, regulatory importance and dependencies.

**Where does quantum enter?** — Quantum optimization selects the best combination of remediation actions under budget and other constraints.

**What does the customer receive?** — A clear view of what is risky, why it is risky, how much it could cost, and what to fix first.

**Is it a dashboard?** — Yes, the dashboard is the main interface.

**Risk report?** — Yes, it can generate an evidence-backed risk view/report.

**Remediation plan?** — Yes, it produces a prioritized remediation plan.

**Optimization result?** — Yes, it shows the optimal remediation portfolio from the quantum-classical optimization layer.

**Compliance evidence?** — Yes, the goal is to link findings back to their supporting regulatory and asset evidence.

**Insurance score?** — Potentially later; the Q-Risk score could become an input for cyber-insurance risk intelligence, but we should not claim this as a current regulated insurance score.
