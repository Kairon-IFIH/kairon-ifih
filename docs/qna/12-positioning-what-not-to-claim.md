# 12. Positioning — What We Should NOT Claim

KAIRON should NOT claim that asset discovery, GRC, regulatory intelligence, cyber-risk quantification, or quantum-readiness are individually new. The differentiation is the combination: discover assets → map regulation/risk → quantify financial exposure → optimize the remediation portfolio under a real budget.

For example, Deloitte already offers quantum-cyber readiness and cryptographic inventory; Qinsight and CipherReady also address cryptographic discovery/readiness; and cyber-risk quantification vendors already translate risk into financial terms.
