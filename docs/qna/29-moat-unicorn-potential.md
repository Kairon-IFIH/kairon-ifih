# 29. Moat / Unicorn Potential

**What gets stronger with every new customer?** — Our risk models, benchmarks, integrations and understanding of real remediation outcomes.

**Do we accumulate anonymized benchmarks?** — Yes, with explicit customer permission and appropriate anonymization.

**Do we develop a proprietary risk graph?** — Yes, the Asset → Regulation → Risk → Financial Impact → Action graph can become a core proprietary asset.

**Do we develop proprietary optimization formulations?** — Yes, our financial-risk remediation optimization models can become increasingly specialized.

**Do we build insurer relationships?** — Yes, as a later expansion into cyber-risk intelligence.

**Do we build regulator relationships?** — Yes, through appropriate pilots, sandbox pathways and ecosystem engagement.

**Do we build a regulatory knowledge graph?** — Yes, connecting regulations to assets, controls and risks is part of our long-term platform.

**Do we create industry benchmarks?** — Yes, eventually through permissioned and anonymized ecosystem data.

**Do we become part of institutions' risk workflows?** — That is the goal: KAIRON becomes part of their recurring risk, compliance and capital-allocation process.

**How difficult is switching away from KAIRON?** — Over time, switching becomes harder because KAIRON can become deeply integrated with the institution's asset inventory, regulatory mappings, risk history and remediation decisions.
