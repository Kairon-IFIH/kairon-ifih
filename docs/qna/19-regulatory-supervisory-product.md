# 19. Regulatory / Supervisory Product

**Is IFSCA our customer?** — Not initially; our first paying customers are regulated financial institutions.

**Who is our customer?** — Banks and financial institutions operating in GIFT IFSC.

**Could IFSCA eventually use KAIRON?** — Yes, potentially as a sector-level risk-intelligence tool, subject to approval and governance.

**What would a regulator see?** — Aggregated risk trends, not private customer data by default.

**Entity-level or sector-level?** — Primarily sector-level for regulators; institution-level access would require authorization.

**Would data be anonymized?** — Yes, any cross-institution benchmarking would be permissioned and anonymized.

**Would institutions voluntarily share it?** — Only with explicit consent and a clear governance framework.

**What governance is needed?** — Strong data ownership, access control, privacy, auditability and regulatory oversight.

**What is the sandbox route?** — We would explore the appropriate IFSCA FinTech Sandbox or Innovation Sandbox pathway.

**What approvals are needed?** — Any required approvals would be determined with IFSCA based on our final product and activities.

**Are we a regulated entity?** — Not necessarily; initially we position KAIRON as a B2B technology provider to regulated entities.

**Are we providing regulated advice?** — No, we provide technology-based risk decision support.

**Are we providing legal advice?** — No, regulatory outputs are informational and require human compliance review.

**Are we an auditor?** — No, KAIRON provides evidence and analytics but does not replace an independent auditor.

**Are we an insurer?** — No, insurance is a potential future partnership/business channel.

**Are we a rating agency?** — No, Q-Risk is our internal proprietary risk score, not an official rating.
