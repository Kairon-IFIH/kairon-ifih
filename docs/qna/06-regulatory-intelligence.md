# 6. Regulatory Intelligence

**Which regulations do we support in MVP?** — Start with IFSCA/India + DPDP, then add selected EU and US frameworks.

**Why these?** — They cover our initial GIFT customer and give us a path toward global expansion.

**Are they officially sourced?** — Yes, we use documents from official regulator/government sources wherever possible.

**Where does regulatory data come from?** — Directly from official regulations, circulars, guidelines and published regulatory documents.

**How do we verify the source?** — Every regulatory answer is linked back to its original official document.

**How often do we update it?** — We continuously track regulatory updates and refresh the knowledge base when official changes are published.

**How do we handle regulatory changes?** — We version the documents and update the affected requirements and risk mappings.

**How do we handle different versions?** — We keep the document version and effective date so the system knows which requirement applied when.

**How do we handle effective dates?** — Each requirement is mapped to its applicable effective date wherever the source provides one.

**How do we determine applicability?** — We use the institution's jurisdiction, entity type, business activity, asset and data context.

**How do we avoid hallucinations?** — KAIRON answers only from verified regulatory sources and flags anything it cannot substantiate.

**Can users see the exact source?** — Yes, users can trace the recommendation back to the relevant regulation and section.

**Can users trace a risk back to a regulation?** — Yes, the chain is Regulation → Requirement → Asset → Control Gap → Risk.

**How do we distinguish law from guidance?** — We clearly label whether the source is a regulation, guideline, circular, standard or best practice.

**How do we handle conflicting regulations?** — We flag the conflict and require human compliance review rather than making an unsupported decision.

**How do we handle overlapping regulations?** — KAIRON maps multiple applicable requirements to the same asset or control to identify overlap and combined obligations.

**How do we handle institution-specific applicability?** — The system considers the institution's jurisdiction, license/entity type, business activity and data handled.

**Is KAIRON giving legal advice?** — No, KAIRON provides regulatory intelligence and decision support, not legal advice.

**How do we communicate that?** — Every high-impact regulatory recommendation is clearly marked as AI-assisted and subject to human compliance approval.

**Who reviews regulatory interpretations?** — A qualified compliance/legal professional at the customer institution remains responsible for final interpretation and approval.

**Why should a bank trust KAIRON?** — Because every important answer is source-backed, traceable, versioned and reviewable by a human — not a black-box AI answer.
