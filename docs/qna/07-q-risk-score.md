# 7. Q-Risk / Regulatory Risk Assurance Score

**What exactly is the score?** — Q-Risk is KAIRON's 0–100 overall risk score showing how exposed an institution is.

**What does 100 mean?** — 100 means highest overall risk exposure, not "100% non-compliant."

**What does 0 mean?** — 0 means minimal measured exposure based on the factors we assess.

**What contributes to the score?** — Cyber risk, regulatory exposure, asset criticality, control gaps, third-party risk and quantum-readiness.

**Why these factors?** — Because together they show how exposed the institution is and how much that exposure could matter financially.

**How are the weights decided?** — We start with a transparent methodology and validate/refine the weights with industry and regulatory experts.

**Are the weights configurable?** — Yes, institutions can adjust weights based on their own risk appetite and priorities.

**Is it regulatory or proprietary?** — It is KAIRON's proprietary risk score, not an official regulatory score.

**Can customers understand why their score changed?** — Yes, KAIRON shows which assets, risks or control gaps caused the change.

**Can we show evidence behind every score?** — Yes, every major score component should be traceable to underlying asset, control and regulatory evidence.

**Can the score be benchmarked?** — Yes, eventually we can provide anonymized benchmarking across comparable institutions.

**Can it be compared across institutions?** — Yes, but only using appropriate anonymized and normalized data.

**Can customers use it internally?** — Yes, mainly for risk prioritization, management reporting and budget allocation.

**Can insurers use it?** — Potentially yes, as risk intelligence for cyber-insurance underwriting, subject to validation and insurer acceptance.

**Can regulators use it?** — Potentially, but only through an appropriate regulatory arrangement and governance framework.

**What if someone disagrees with the score?** — They can inspect the underlying factors, challenge the assumptions and adjust the inputs or methodology.

**How often is it recalculated?** — It should update whenever important asset, control, regulatory or risk information changes.

**What data affects it?** — Asset inventory, vulnerabilities, controls, regulatory obligations, business criticality, financial exposure, third-party risk and quantum-readiness.

**Why should I believe your risk score?** — Because it is transparent, evidence-backed and explainable rather than a black-box number, and we would validate the methodology with real industry data and experts before production.
