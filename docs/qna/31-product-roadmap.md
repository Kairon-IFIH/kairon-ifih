# 31. Product Roadmap

**What is the MVP?** — Asset discovery + regulatory mapping + risk scoring + financial-risk calculation + quantum/classical remediation optimization.

**What did we build tonight?** — A working prototype that demonstrates the full flow from asset/risk data → financial exposure → optimized remediation decision.

**What is intentionally fake?** — The prototype uses synthetic data and simulated enterprise integrations where real customer data is unavailable.

**What is missing?** — Real bank integrations, production-grade scanning, larger datasets, regulatory validation and real-world customer testing.

**What would we build next week?** — Connect KAIRON to real security/data sources and improve the risk and optimization models.

**What would we build in 3 months?** — A pilot-ready product with real asset discovery, regulatory mapping and a validated quantum-classical optimization benchmark.

**What would we build in 6 months?** — Deploy pilots with GIFT financial institutions and build continuous monitoring, reporting and enterprise integrations.

**What would we build in 12 months?** — A production-ready platform with multiple financial-institution customers, stronger optimization and an initial cyber-insurance intelligence layer.

**What requires customer access?** — Real asset data, security posture, business criticality and remediation-cost information.

**What requires regulatory validation?** — Our regulatory mappings, risk-assurance methodology and any future use of KAIRON by regulated entities or regulators.

**What requires real quantum hardware?** — Only proving practical quantum advantage at scale; the prototype can be developed and benchmarked on simulators.

**What requires insurance partnerships?** — Using KAIRON's risk intelligence for underwriting, pricing or cyber-insurance decisions.
