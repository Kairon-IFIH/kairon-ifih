# 34. Why Now?

**Why is this problem urgent today?** — Financial institutions face growing cyber threats, regulatory complexity and increasingly expensive risk decisions.

**What changed recently?** — Financial systems became more digital, interconnected and cross-border while the cost of a security or compliance failure increased.

**Regulatory complexity?** — Institutions increasingly need to manage overlapping requirements across jurisdictions.

**Cloud adoption?** — More cloud and digital infrastructure means more assets to discover, monitor and secure.

**Cross-border finance?** — Cross-border institutions must manage different regulatory and security requirements simultaneously.

**Cyber threats?** — Attack surfaces are growing while financial institutions remain high-value targets.

**Quantum-readiness?** — Institutions need to understand where current cryptography is vulnerable and prioritize future PQC migration.

**Insurance pressure?** — Better, continuously measured cyber-risk information can potentially improve how institutions and insurers understand cyber exposure.

**GIFT IFSC expansion?** — GIFT gives us a concentrated ecosystem of international financial activity where this problem can be validated.

**AI maturity?** — AI now makes it practical to connect large amounts of regulatory, asset and risk information into a usable decision system.

**Availability of quantum simulators?** — We can develop and benchmark the quantum optimization layer today without waiting for large-scale fault-tolerant quantum hardware.

**Regulatory sandbox availability?** — A sandbox pathway can give us a structured way to test an innovative financial-services solution with appropriate regulatory oversight.

## The one-line answer to memorize

> "Financial institutions now face more digital assets, more cross-border regulation and more expensive cyber risk, while AI and quantum technologies have reached a point where we can start building a better risk-decision layer today."
