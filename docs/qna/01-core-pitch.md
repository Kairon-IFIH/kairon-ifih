# 1. Core Pitch

**What problem are we solving?** — Financial institutions struggle to identify which cyber and regulatory risks to fix first when their budget is limited.

**Who pays us?** — CISOs, CROs and compliance teams of regulated financial institutions, starting with GIFT IFSC.

**Why do they pay us?** — Because KAIRON shows them where to spend their limited security budget to reduce the most financial risk.

**Why can't existing tools solve it?** — Existing tools identify risks, but KAIRON connects those risks to money and optimizes which actions to take first.

**What exactly is quantum optimizing?** — It finds the best combination of risk-remediation actions that gives maximum risk reduction within a fixed budget and regulatory constraints.

**What data goes into the quantum model?** — Asset criticality, risk level, financial exposure, remediation cost, regulatory priority, risk reduction and dependencies.

**Why GIFT IFSC?** — GIFT gives us direct access to an international financial ecosystem and the regulatory/sandbox environment to validate KAIRON with regulated institutions.

**How do we make money?** — We charge financial institutions an annual enterprise SaaS fee, then expand into advanced risk analytics and cyber-insurance intelligence.

**Why are we the team to build it?** — We combine quantum, cybersecurity, post-quantum cryptography and hands-on technology-building experience.

**What happens if KAIRON is wrong?** — KAIRON provides recommendations with evidence and validation, but high-impact decisions always require human approval from the financial institution.
