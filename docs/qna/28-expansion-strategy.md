# 28. Expansion Strategy

**GIFT IFSC → India?** — Yes, after proving the model in GIFT, expand to major Indian financial institutions.

**India → Singapore?** — Yes, Singapore is a logical next market because of its strong international financial ecosystem.

**India → Dubai?** — Yes, Dubai can be another expansion market for cross-border financial services.

**India → Europe?** — Yes, once our regulatory engine supports the required European frameworks.

**India → US?** — Yes, after adapting to US regulatory and enterprise requirements.

**Which jurisdiction is next?** — India first, then Singapore or Dubai based on customer demand and regulatory access.

**Why?** — They are strong international financial centres where our cross-border risk problem remains relevant.

**What changes regulatory-wise?** — The underlying platform stays the same, but the regulatory knowledge, controls and compliance mappings change.

**Can the same architecture support multiple jurisdictions?** — Yes, because regulations are designed as a modular intelligence layer on top of the same risk engine.

**What becomes the common global layer?** — Asset discovery + risk quantification + financial exposure + remediation optimization.
