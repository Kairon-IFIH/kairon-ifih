# 10. What exactly goes into QAOA?

**Cost?** — How much implementing the action costs.

**Risk reduction?** — How much risk the action is expected to remove.

**Expected-loss reduction?** — How much estimated financial exposure the action can reduce.

**Regulatory priority?** — How important or mandatory the action is from a regulatory perspective.

**Asset criticality?** — How important the affected asset is to the institution.

**Operational disruption?** — How much business disruption implementing the action could cause.

**Dependencies?** — Which actions must happen before another action can work.

**Quantum-readiness impact?** — How much the action improves the institution's readiness for future quantum threats.

**Implementation timeline?** — How long the action takes to implement.

## The one answer to memorize

> "Our QAOA model takes the cost, risk reduction, financial impact, regulatory priority and dependencies of possible remediation actions, and finds the combination that minimizes residual financial risk within the institution's available budget and constraints."
