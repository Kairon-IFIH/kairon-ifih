# 17. Cyber Insurance Business

**Why would an insurer care about KAIRON?** — Because KAIRON can provide continuously updated, evidence-backed information about an institution's cyber and regulatory risk.

**What information can we provide insurers?** — Asset exposure, control maturity, risk score, financial exposure, remediation progress and risk trends.

**Is the score standardized?** — Not initially; Q-Risk is our proprietary methodology and would need validation before being used for underwriting.

**Is it auditable?** — It should be, because every score should have evidence, assumptions and an audit trail.

**Is it continuously updated?** — That is the intended product model as new asset and risk data arrives.

**Can insurers compare institutions?** — Potentially, using normalized and permissioned data.

**Can it support underwriting?** — Potentially, as decision-support — not as an automatic underwriting authority.

**Can it support risk improvement?** — Yes, this is actually the more natural first insurance use case.

**Can it help price cyber insurance?** — Potentially, but only after insurer validation and appropriate actuarial/regulatory acceptance.

**Can it detect deterioration in risk?** — Yes, continuous monitoring can flag material changes.

**Can it provide evidence during claims?** — Potentially, through historical risk and control evidence, but this requires insurer/legal validation.

**Who pays us?** — Initially the financial institution; later potentially insurers as a separate B2B2B revenue stream.

**Bank?** — Yes, primary initial customer.

**Insurer?** — Potential future customer.

**Both?** — Potentially, but we should avoid creating conflicts of interest and clearly separate customer permissions and data.

**B2B or B2B2B?** — Core product = B2B SaaS; insurance expansion = B2B2B.

**What regulatory issues arise?** — We must avoid presenting Q-Risk as an official rating, underwriting decision or regulatory certification without the required approvals and validation.

## "Why would an insurer trust KAIRON?"

> "We wouldn't ask an insurer to trust a black-box score; we'd provide transparent methodology, underlying evidence, historical performance and independent validation, and let the insurer decide how much weight KAIRON deserves in underwriting."
