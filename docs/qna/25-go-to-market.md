# 25. Go-To-Market

**How do we get our first customer?** — Start with a GIFT IFSC financial institution through a pilot with its CISO/CRO team.

**Do we approach banks directly?** — Yes, we directly approach the relevant risk, cybersecurity and compliance decision-makers.

**Through the GIFT ecosystem?** — Yes, GIFT is our first ecosystem for introductions, pilots and partnerships.

**Through an accelerator?** — Yes, accelerators can help us get introductions and credibility, but they are not our main sales channel.

**Through regulatory sandbox?** — Yes, where appropriate, we can explore the IFSCA sandbox pathway to validate the product.

**Through cybersecurity partners?** — Yes, security companies can help us reach institutions and integrate with their existing tools.

**Through auditors?** — Potentially, auditors and compliance firms can become channel or implementation partners.

**Through insurers?** — Later, insurers can become partners for our cyber-risk intelligence offering.

**Who introduces us?** — Ideally GIFT ecosystem partners, mentors, cybersecurity partners and early design partners introduce us to decision-makers.

**What is our sales cycle?** — Start with a 90-day pilot, then convert successful pilots into annual enterprise contracts.

**How long does enterprise onboarding take?** — Initially around 4–12 weeks, depending on integrations, security review and data access.

**What is the pilot?** — We take a limited set of the institution's assets and demonstrate discovery → risk → financial exposure → optimized remediation.

**What is the minimum product needed for a pilot?** — Asset discovery, regulatory mapping, risk scoring, financial-risk calculation and the quantum-classical remediation optimizer.

**What converts pilot → paid contract?** — Proving measurable risk reduction, clear financial value and a workflow the customer wants to continue using.

**What proves ROI?** — We show how much risk/financial exposure was reduced compared with the cost of the recommended remediation.
