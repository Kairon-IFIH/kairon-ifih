# 26. First 3 Customers

**First target customer?** — A regulated GIFT IFSC bank with a large cyber and compliance footprint.

**Second target customer?** — A large fintech/financial-services company operating under a regulated framework.

**Third target customer?** — A financial institution/insurer that needs continuous cyber-risk and regulatory intelligence.

**Why would customer #1 buy?** — They need to prioritize large numbers of risks while working with limited budgets.

**Why would customer #2 buy?** — Fintechs need a faster way to manage growing cyber and regulatory complexity.

**Why would customer #3 buy?** — They can use KAIRON's risk intelligence for better risk assessment and eventually insurance decisions.

**What do they have in common?** — They handle valuable financial assets, sensitive data and strict regulatory requirements.

**Which customer gives us the best data?** — The institution with the most mature asset and security infrastructure gives us the richest initial data.

**Which gives credibility?** — A recognized regulated financial institution gives us the strongest credibility.

**Which gives revenue?** — A large bank or financial institution gives us the strongest enterprise revenue opportunity.

**Which gives distribution?** — GIFT ecosystem partners and established financial institutions can help us reach additional customers.
