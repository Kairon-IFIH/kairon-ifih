# 20. Trust, Liability & Wrong Outputs

**What if KAIRON gives the wrong recommendation?** — The system flags uncertainty and requires human validation for high-impact decisions.

**Who is responsible?** — We are responsible for our technology; the institution remains responsible for its final business and regulatory decisions.

**What does KAIRON guarantee?** — We guarantee the agreed platform functionality, security controls and transparency — not the outcome of every risk decision.

**What does KAIRON NOT guarantee?** — We do not guarantee regulatory compliance, zero cyber incidents or perfect predictions.

**Who makes the final regulatory decision?** — The institution's authorized compliance/risk function.

**Can recommendations auto-execute?** — High-impact recommendations should not auto-execute.

**Which decisions require human approval?** — Regulatory, financial and high-risk remediation decisions.

**What if AI confidence is low?** — KAIRON flags the result and sends it for human review.

**What if regulatory evidence conflicts?** — KAIRON highlights the conflict instead of making an unsupported conclusion.

**What if quantum gives an infeasible solution?** — We reject it and fall back to a validated classical solution.

**What if quantum performs worse than classical?** — We use the better validated result and report the benchmark honestly.

**What if asset inventory is incomplete?** — KAIRON flags the data gap and reduces confidence in the risk assessment.

**Can the system show uncertainty?** — Yes, every important recommendation should include confidence and supporting evidence.

**Are decisions logged?** — Yes, recommendations, evidence, approvals and changes should be audit-logged.

**Can an auditor reconstruct a decision?** — Yes, the goal is full traceability from regulation → asset → risk → recommendation → approval.
