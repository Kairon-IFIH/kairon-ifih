# 33. Failure Scenarios

**What if asset discovery is incomplete?** — KAIRON flags the missing data and lowers confidence instead of pretending the risk picture is complete.

**What if regulatory data is outdated?** — We track official updates and flag outdated or superseded requirements.

**What if AI hallucinates?** — Regulatory answers are source-backed, and important decisions require human review.

**What if quantum is worse than classical?** — We use the better validated classical result and report the quantum result honestly.

**What if the customer has 1 million assets?** — We use scalable ingestion, prioritization and optimization instead of processing everything equally.

**What if regulations conflict?** — KAIRON flags the conflict and sends it for compliance review rather than guessing.

**What if the institution doesn't have enough data?** — We start with available data, clearly identify gaps and avoid overconfident conclusions.

**What if the customer doesn't trust the score?** — We show exactly how the score was calculated and provide the underlying evidence.

**What if insurers don't accept the score?** — Insurance is a future expansion, so the core KAIRON business remains independent of insurer adoption.

**What if a bank already has a GRC solution?** — We integrate with it rather than replace it; KAIRON becomes the decision layer above existing tools.

**What if Microsoft, AWS or Google builds this?** — Our defense is deep financial-institution workflows, regulatory mapping, risk-to-money models and customer-specific optimization data.

**What if a large cybersecurity vendor copies us?** — Our long-term moat is the accumulated risk, remediation and financial-outcome data, not just the software.

**What if quantum advantage never materializes?** — KAIRON still works as a classical optimization platform; quantum is an additional path to future advantage, not a dependency for the business.
