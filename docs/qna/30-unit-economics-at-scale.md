# 30. Unit Economics at Scale

**What does one customer cost to onboard?** — Initially, around ₹5–10 lakh for integration, setup and security configuration; we aim to reduce this with standard integrations.

**What does one customer cost to serve?** — Mainly cloud, AI/quantum compute, monitoring and customer support, which should decrease per customer as we scale.

**What is gross margin?** — We target 70–85% at scale because KAIRON is primarily enterprise software, but this needs validation with real operating costs.

**What is ARR/customer?** — Our initial working assumption is around ₹25–50 lakh ARR per enterprise customer, to be validated through pilots.

**What is CAC?** — We don't have a proven CAC yet; initially it will be driven mainly by enterprise sales, partnerships and pilots.

**What is LTV?** — LTV will come from ARR × gross margin × customer lifetime, and we will refine it once we have real retention data.

**What happens to margins when customer count increases?** — Margins should improve because software and infrastructure costs don't grow as fast as customer revenue.

**Is the product software-heavy enough?** — Yes, the core product is SaaS; implementation and consulting should become a smaller part over time.

**Are services becoming too large?** — No, services should mainly support onboarding and integration rather than become the main revenue source.

**Can regulatory content updates be automated?** — Yes, we can automate monitoring and ingestion of official regulatory updates, with human validation for important changes.

**Can deployment be standardized?** — Yes, through reusable APIs, connectors, templates and predefined onboarding workflows.
